Our Mission

Protecting the Spirit of the Game

These six values act as a shield — protecting fair-play, respect and positive behavior on and off the pitch.

By shifting focus from winning at all costs to winning with principles, SHIELD aims to reshape the football culture at every level — creating a game that is fairer, safer and more inspiring for all.

The SHIELD Medallion is more than recognition. It highlights integrity and leadership, sets a visible example for players, and reinforces positive behavior across teams, coaches, and families.

The Process

How It Works

Straightforward, fair, transparent and built for clubs worldwide.

1

Post-Match Selection

After every match, captains and the referee select one player from the opposing team who best embodied the SHIELD values.

2

Medallion Awarded

The chosen player receives a SHIELD Medallion in its own dedicated, high-quality display box.

3

Digitally Tracked

The award is logged through a simple digital tracking system, building a transparent record of recognition.

4

Milestone Unlocks

Players collect their boxes and unlock milestone recognitions at 5, 10 and 15 medallions.

5

Role Model Status

Consistent leaders are celebrated as SHIELD role models — inspiring the next generation of principled players.

Programs

Two Paths, One Mission

Whether you're nurturing young talent or competing at the highest level, there's a SHIELD program for your club.

Youth & Amateur

Youth SHIELD

Designed for youth teams and amateur clubs. This affordable version focuses on teaching values early. Players receive one dedicated display box with each medallion earned from the opposing team. Progress is tracked digitally and those who reach key milestones receive special recognition as emerging leaders.

Senior & Professional

The SHIELD

Aimed at senior and professional teams. Every medallion earned comes with a dedicated box, and across the season, the league compiles a final ranking of players who have received the most medallions. This adds visibility and prestige, highlighting role models who consistently demonstrate fair play.

About Us

The People Behind SHIELD

Founded by people who believe football deserves better.

JD

Johan Djourou

Partner & Co-Founder

Former Swiss international centre-back who played for over a decade — including stints with Arsenal in the Premier League. Today, as coach of Lancy FC's women's first team and technical director of the Swiss women's national teams, Johan actively implements SHIELD, bringing leadership and integrity to reward sportsmanship at the grassroots level.

EJ

Ella Joanes

Partner & Co-Founder

Co-founder of SHIELD, Ella originated the concept and developed the on-pitch recognition system — making sportsmanship, integrity and respect visible and meaningful for players of all ages. Together with Johan, she is leading SHIELD's development and expansion with the ambition to reshape football culture from grassroots to elite level.
